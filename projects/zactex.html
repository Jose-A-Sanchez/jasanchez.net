ZAC-TEX Construction & Roofing

A complete web presence for a family-owned construction and roofing company in Texas: website, domain, DNS security, and business email.

zactexllc.com

The problem

The business had no web presence and no professional email. Customers in Dallas and Houston had no way to find it online, and the owner was running everything from a personal inbox.

What I built

  • A bilingual website with an English/Spanish toggle, a full-screen photo hero, and pages for services, service areas, and contact
  • Hosting on Cloudflare Workers, deployed from a GitHub repository so every change is versioned
  • DNS on Cloudflare with DNSSEC, the web application firewall, and the CDN turned on
  • Business email on Microsoft Exchange Online, so the company has a real address instead of a personal one
  • A Google Business Profile so the company shows up in local search

Why plain HTML

The site doesn't need a database or a CMS, and the owner won't be editing it himself. Static files on Cloudflare load fast, cost nothing to host, and leave almost nothing to patch or attack.

What's next

A working contact form, real job site photos to replace stock images, and a finished About page.
